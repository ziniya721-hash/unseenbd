Legal & Ethical Framework

Privacy & Ethical Travel Charter

Our commitment to user privacy, intellectual property rights of local visual artists, and environmental stewardship across Bangladesh.

Data Protection

1. Privacy Policy

Hidden Bangladesh values your privacy and practices data minimization. When you interact with our website:

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Your email address is collected strictly to deliver our monthly dispatches and seasonal travel recommendations. We will never sell, rent, or distribute your email to third parties.
  • Inquiry Forms: Information submitted via our contact form is used solely by our editorial team to respond to your specific guide or contribution inquiry.
  • Browser Storage: Saved wishlist items and audio preferences are stored exclusively on your device via HTML5 Local Storage. No tracking cookies are used to monitor external browsing behavior.
Artist Protection

2. Photography & Intellectual Property

All photographs exhibited on Hidden Bangladesh remain the intellectual property of their respective creators.

  • Photographer names and attribution are prominently displayed in all full-screen lightbox views and captions.
  • Commercial duplication, resale, or unauthorized redistribution of high-resolution images without explicit written consent from the credited photographer is strictly prohibited.
  • If you represent a media outlet seeking high-resolution licensing of any featured work, please contact our team for artist licensing referrals.
Environmental Responsibility

3. Traveler Code of Conduct

Travelers referencing Hidden Bangladesh guides agree to practice responsible exploration:

  • Zero Waste Policy: Always carry your plastic waste, bottles, and food containers out of remote wetlands (Tanguar Haor), mountain peaks (Marayan Thong, Keokradong), and coastal sanctuaries (Saint Martin's Island).
  • Cultural Dignity: Treat indigenous traditions, religious sanctuaries, and ethnic hill communities with unreserved respect. Always seek consent prior to photography.
  • Wildlife Sanctuary Rules: Avoid amplified sound equipment, loud boat motors in freshwater swamp forests, or disturbing nesting waterbirds and mangrove animals in the Sundarbans.

Technical Implementation Notice

This platform is crafted as a fully functional, zero-dependency static web application using semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 variables, and modular vanilla JavaScript. Forms and search run client-side with full instant feedback and local state persistence. For full-scale production deployment, this frontend architecture can be connected seamlessly to a headless CMS (e.g., Strapi, Sanity, Ghost) and serverless backend API (e.g., Supabase, Node/Express, or Cloudflare Workers).
