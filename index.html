Our Manifesto

Slow Travel & Ethical Stewardship

Hidden Bangladesh exists to protect what we celebrate. We advocate for zero plastic in fragile wetlands, fair wages for local indigenous guides, cultural dignity for ethnic minorities, and quiet respect for delicate ecosystems.

Read Our Ethical Travel Charter
01.

Leave No Trace

Strict waste containment on haors, peaks, and coral shores.

02.

Local Direct

Empowering native boatmen, indigenous hosts, and artisans directly.

03.

Deep Context

Understanding historical lore, river geography, and tribal heritage.

04.

Zero Exploitation

Respectful photography with prior consent for all portraits.
